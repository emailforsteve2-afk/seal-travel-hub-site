1. What this app is

SEAL Travel Hub is a day-by-day itinerary that stays on the device first. You plan flights, hotels, trains, cruises, meals, and notes. Premium adds sharing with other travelers, family at home, photos on the trip, a journal, packing, and cloud sync.

The itinerary is not a booking engine. You still buy tickets with the airline, hotel, or cruise line. This app holds the plan, the pictures, and the notes.

Times are wall-clock times at that place. They are not converted to whoever opens the trip.

3. Basic and Premium

The same traveler app starts in Basic (no account). Signing in creates a login. Premium is a license on that login (admin grant or a one-time code until store billing exists). Login and paid access are not the same thing.

Basic (unsigned)

  • Private itinerary on this phone or this browser only.
  • You own every trip you create. View | Edit is available.
  • Paste a confirmation uses a built-in reader on the device (not the AI extractor). No trip email address.
  • Profile (personal, travel programs, medical) stays on this device.
  • Optional lock-screen reminders on the phone.
  • Home heading: AS Travel App (basic). About shows Basic x.y.z (for example Basic 1.0.11).

Browser and phone are not connected in Basic. There is no export, Bluetooth, QR, or other unsigned path to copy a trip between them. To move a browser-only trip to a phone: Sign in or create account on that browser, then sign in on the phone and wait until Online.

Hidden in Basic (not grayed out): invites, family/friend viewers, forward-to-email import, AI extract, flight status overlay, Nearby, trip photos, journal, packing/prep/usuals, print, connection badge, Travelers list, Sign out.

Signed in, still Basic

The login can sync trips you are on. Premium tools stay off until a grant or code. Menu shows Upgrade to Premium.

Premium (signed in with a Premium license)

Cloud sync, invites, inbound email + AI extract, print, Nearby on phones, trip photos on phones, journal on phone and web, prep/packing/usuals, lock-screen notices for shared photos/comments/journal, connection badge.

After sign-out

This device is Basic again. Profile details stay so Personal is not blank. Itinerary rows already downloaded can stay. Sign in later with the same email and password on this device: with internet you reach the cloud; without internet the phone can unlock that saved account so trips already here (and Nearby) can work. A new email needs internet at least once. New unsigned trips after sign-out are local to this install.

5. Accounts

Create account or sign in

Name, email, and password. Use the same email you were invited with so the trip can attach. If the project requires email confirmation, confirm, then sign in.

Look for invitations on an empty Premium home pulls pending invites for this email.

Forgot password

From sign-in, request an email link. On a phone the link is meant to open the app. On the web it returns to the website. Then choose a new password.

Change email or password

Profile while signed in. Changing email may need a confirmation link; the old address can remain the login until you open it.

Upgrade to Premium

Menu → Upgrade to Premium. Enter a one-time code from the product administrator (store purchase is not in the app yet).

Connection badge (Premium)

Online, Downloading… / Uploading…, or Offline. Until Online, a phone only shows trips already stored there. Retry sync if it stays offline when you expect a connection.

Delete my account

Menu → Delete my account. This permanently removes your login, traveler profile (including passport and medical), and every trip you own. Trips you do not own stay for the other people; you are removed. This cannot be undone. You must be able to reach the server.

7. View and Edit

Owners and editors open a trip in Viewing. Tap View | Edit to Editing to change days, items, trip facts, or import. Viewers and family/friend viewers never see that switch.

Background work (keeping days in sync, trip mailbox) follows permission, not the switch.

Search is in View only (hidden in Edit so import and day editing stay uncluttered).

8. Create and change a trip

New trip

  1. Your trips → New trip.
  2. Title (required).
  3. Destination (optional).
  4. Type: General, Cruise, Train, Road trip, or Flight.
  5. Start and end dates (optional, MM/DD/YYYY). Dates create the day list.

You become owner. The trip is saved on the device immediately and syncs when Premium is online. Cruise or Train type does not change how calendar days are generated; it unlocks Cruise Day / Train Day and ship or train times on each day.

Edit trip facts

In Edit, Tap Here (or the trip header) to change title, destination, type, and dates. Changing dates rebuilds the day list. Days outside the new range are removed; items on those days become Not on a day yet.

Archived trips

Why this exists. Your trips is meant for trips you still use: upcoming, in progress, or ones you open often. After you get home, that card still occupies the list. Archive tucks a finished (or unused) trip out of the way so home stays short, while you keep the itinerary as a record.

What it does. Archive hides the trip from Your trips for you. Days, people, journal, photos, packing, and invites stay. Other people on the trip still see it on their home lists. You can restore it later, print it, or open it again as a memory of the trip.

What it is not. Archive is not delete. It does not cancel airline or hotel bookings. It does not remove the trip for anyone else. Delete trip (owner only) is the action that removes the itinerary for everyone.

Who can archive. Owners and editors, in Edit. Traveler-viewers and family/friend viewers do not see Archive trip (they have no Edit switch). Each archive is personal: if two editors both archive, each hides it only from their own home.

How to archive. Open the trip → View | Edit → Archive trip at the bottom of the days list. Confirm. The trip leaves Your trips immediately.

How to restore. Menu (three lines) → Archived trips. Empty state: No archived trips. If there are any, Tap a trip to restore it to Your trips. You can also tap Restore trip on the days list if you still have that trip open.

Value in practice. Keep last year’s cruise without mixing it with next month’s flights. Hide a trip you were invited to but are not using. Keep a copy of the plan after travel without deleting shared photos and journal notes.

Delete trip

Owner, in Edit, with confirmation. The trip and itinerary are removed for everyone on it.

9. Load bookings (paste, forward, AI extract)

You can type the itinerary, paste a confirmation, forward mail to the trip, or mix all three. You do not need an agency.

Type it

In Edit, open a day or Add itinerary item. See Itinerary items.

Paste (Import Booking Confirmation Email)

  1. Switch to Edit.
  2. Tap Import Booking Confirmation Email.
  3. Paste the email, confirmation, or sailing itinerary.
  4. Run extract. Review flights, legs, times, names.
  5. Save. Nothing is written until you confirm.

Premium and Online: extract uses the AI itinerary extractor. Offline, Basic, or if AI is unavailable: a built-in reader on the device. Always check the draft.

Forward to the trip address (Premium)

Each editable trip has a unique inbound address (shown in Edit under Forward a confirmation).

  1. Copy address.
  2. Forward the entire airline, hotel, train, or cruise email to that address (not a screenshot of the subject).
  3. When it arrives, Booking to review (or Email stored) appears.
  4. Import to review the draft, or Remove if it is the wrong mail.
  5. Undo last import removes the last saved batch if you need to try again.

That address is only for this trip. Family/friend viewers never see it. Detected names can be assigned to items; they are not emailed as invites until you invite them.

10. Days

When a trip has a start date, there is one card per calendar date: weekday, date, Day N (Day 1 = first calendar date).

Place, temperature, currency

The place on a day is personal. Two travelers can see different cities (and matching typical high/low and currency) from *their* flights and stays.

Overnight city comes from the last movement that day: flight / train / coach arrival, hotel stay, or a one-way Drive ending city. That city carries forward until another of those. Round-trip Drive and Car service do not move it.

Hawaii island temperatures can fill in without internet. Other places may use the network only to look up a city—not to store the trip in the cloud. Currency is a label from the place, not a live exchange rate.

On a travel day, the origin of that day’s first real departure (for example PWM → Portland, Maine) can set the city, not a same-day layover.

Day title

In Edit, open a day and tap the facts to set a day title (Travel Day, Beach day, Day trip to Boston). It shows on day cards and the day screen. It is not the overnight city.

Place photo on the day card

When a free-to-use photo is found (Wikimedia Commons or Openverse: CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain). Tap i for creator, source, and license. At Sea uses a cruise-ship photo. Menu → Display → Days-list thumbnails turns the small pictures on the day list on or off. These photos are not the trip album.

On the day: ship arrive / departure / all aboard, or train arrive / depart. Docked or Tender and train overnight/station facts are hidden from family/friend viewers. Journey numbers are optional. Family/friend viewers can read them when set; they cannot edit.

Opening a day

Date, typical high/low, currency, place photo, cruise/train times, then Journal Entry and Photos (Premium) above Activities & Details. Previous day / Next day at the bottom. Back to days returns to the cards.

Wide web: two, three, or four day cards across.

11. Itinerary items

Activities & Details on a day. In Edit, add or tap an item.

Fields (all categories)

  • Title
  • Category: Flight, Hotel, Rental Car, Drive, Car service, Train, Coach/Bus, Cruise, Activity, Dining, Note
  • Start and end date and clock time (labels change: depart/arrive, check-in/out, embark, pickup/drop-off, and so on)
  • Who it is for: everyone, or named travelers (including people still invited). Empty selection means everyone. The day list shows EVERYONE or first names, with NAME (pending) for invites.
  • Confirmation (optional)
  • Location (optional)
  • Notes (optional; never shown to family/friend viewers). On iPhone and Android, tap a number to Call or Copy. Web leaves notes as text.
  • Reminder on the phone for timed types (see Reminders)

A train or cruise trip can still hold flights and hotels on fly-in days. Trip type does not block item categories.

Items can exist Not on a day yet until a start date matches a day.

Overnight cruise, multi-night hotel, and rental-car items use blank clocks on some days so check-in, check-out, pickup, and drop-off read clearly.

Editors can change or delete an item. Viewers and family/friend viewers can read (within their rules) and cannot edit.

Flight

  • Airline (and optional flight number) so a reminder can name the carrier.
  • Legs: origin and destination airport codes, layovers, carrier, flight number, times. The day list can show COS → DEN → OGG.
  • Status overlay: see Flight status overlay.

Hotel

Multi-night stays show clearly on check-in and check-out days.

Rental car

Title often “{Company} Rental Car”. Class, extras, and phones belong in notes.

Drive

One-way requires an ending city; that city is overnight until another movement. Round trip does not move the city.

Car service

Booked car, limo, taxi, or rideshare. Location is pickup → drop-off. Does not change overnight city.

Train, Coach/Bus, Cruise, Activity, Dining, Note

Use the matching category so times, reminders, and day facts line up. Cruise items are different from Cruise trip type.

12. Search

On the trip, in View: Search days and activities. Matches day titles, dates, day numbers, Cruise Day / Train Day text, item titles, categories, locations, confirmation numbers, times, flight paths, and (travelers only) notes. Family/friend viewers do not search notes.

13. Print

While a trip is open (not on Your trips), menu → About this App:

  • Print itinerary — device or browser print dialog. Title, destination, dates, travelers (not shown to family/friend viewers), then each day with place, typical temps, currency, cruise/train facts, and items. Family/friend viewers get the same itinerary they already see (no item notes, no dock/tender). No passport or loyalty. Closing the iPhone sheet without printing is fine. Margins about 0.6 inch.
  • Print journal — every journal note you may see, by day, with the writer’s name. Your Only me notes are on your copy and labeled. Other people’s private notes never appear.

14. Journal

Premium. Phone and website. Lock-screen notices for shared notes are phone only.

Read Journal

On the trip days list. Read-only feed of every note you may see, by day. Filters: All, Mine, Shared. Family/friend viewers see this when notes were shared with family/friends. They do not see Journal Entry on a day.

Journal Entry

On a day, above Activities & Details. Owners and editors write. Traveler-viewers and family/friend viewers do not compose.

  • Only me (default, private)
  • and/or Travelers
  • and/or Family/friends

Last audience is remembered on this device. You can edit or delete your notes. All days returns to Read Journal.

In Edit, Add itinerary item adds a flight or hotel, not a journal note.

15. Trip photos

Premium, iPhone and Android only. Not on the website. Not a public link. Not an Apple Photos / Google Photos album.

Photos on the trip (and on a day) opens the album.

Share (travelers: owner, editor, traveler-viewer)

  1. Check Travelers and/or Family/friends. Last choice is remembered on this phone.
  2. Optional caption (same caption for up to 12 pictures in one batch).
  3. Choose from camera roll.

You do not pick a day. Grouping uses when the photo was taken (or share time if the file has no date). Photos that do not match a trip day sit under Other photos.

If the file includes a location (Camera location on), a place name can appear on its own line. The app does not read live GPS. Day line on the opened photo is day and date, not the day-card city.

Look

Story by Day 1, Day 2, …. Open a picture: place name, caption, day/date. Swipe for the next. Anyone who can see it can Save to camera roll. The sharer can Edit caption. The sharer, or an owner/editor, can Remove. Travelers and family/friend viewers who can see the picture can comment. Thumbnails show a comment count (teal if unread). Photos with new comments sit at the top.

Opening a trip can popup for new photos or comments. Display → Notice for new Photos turns that off. Lock screen for Photos, comments, and journal is separate (phone).

Family/friend viewers see only shares that included family/friends or both. They cannot share.

16. Travel Prep Reminders

Premium, travelers only (not family/friend viewers). Menu while a trip is open. In-app checkboxes. No lock-screen alerts.

  • This trip — shared chores (hold mail, trip insurance, rental-car insurance). Owners and editors check these off.
  • For you — your reminders (passport, prescription refill). Only you check these off.

The app suggests a few from the itinerary. Add from a short catalog, type your own, or Remove a line. Suggestions are not passport, visa, or insurance advice. Done returns to the menu.

17. Packing List

Premium, travelers only. Menu while a trip is open.

Days and nights for this trip show at the top. Each traveler has Carry-On Bag and Checked Bag. Tap a bag to switch. Passport / ID, medications, chargers, and toiletries start in carry-on; clothes start in checked. Move to Carry-On / Move to Checked under an item.

On a narrow phone, the name is on the first line; Target, Packed, and Complete/Missing on the second.

Each line:

  • Name
  • Target — how many you plan to bring (+/− or tap the number)
  • Packed — how many are in that luggage
  • Complete or Missing
  • × removes the line from this trip (not from your catalog)

A Missing from… note lists anything still short in that bag.

Add Item — type a name, pick a category (Documents, Clothes, Toiletries, Health, Electronics, Activities, Kids, Other). The app may pre-select (dental floss → Health). That name is saved to your catalog for later trips unless it already matches a built-in line (for example Socks).

Add from catalog — check built-in lines plus your extras, then Add to packing list. × on an owned extra drops it from your catalog.

My usuals for this bag — Add or Add all … usuals for this bag only. Edit … usuals opens Packing usuals. Add item and Add from catalog do not stay open at the same time.

Kids lists

Owners and editors: at the bottom of packing (under Your bags and any existing kids lists). Enter age → Add kids list (Infant / Toddler / Child / Teen). Clothing counts follow that age. Remove list deletes that kids list and its items; it does not change who is on the trip. Confirm Keep list / Remove list on the page. Your bags cannot be deleted this way. Viewer travelers pack only their own bags.

Prep and packing suggestions are hints, not medical or legal advice.

18. Packing usuals

Premium, travelers only. Menu → Packing usuals, or Edit carry-on usuals / Edit checked bag usuals from packing.

Separate teal-gray screen. Two lists: Carry-On usuals and Checked usuals, on your account, reused on every trip.

  • Check catalog lines → Save selected to … usuals
  • Or type a name, pick a category, Save to usuals (also adds to your catalog)
  • Delete removes it from that bag’s usuals

On Packing List, with Carry-On selected, Add all carry-on bag usuals adds only carry-on usuals to that bag.

20. Traveler profile

Menu → Profile. This is your wallet of travel identity: who you are on bookings, documents you may need at a counter, and (for you only) health details you want on the phone if something happens away from home.

Tap a header to open a section. From the menu you can also jump straight into Personal, Travel Programs, or Medical.

A booking that uses a nickname still counts as you. Passport, loyalty, and medical are not copied into the shared day-by-day itinerary that family/friend viewers sync.

This app is not a medical device and not a substitute for official cards. Keep originals of passports, visas, insurance cards, and prescriptions.

Personal

  • Photo (phone library, or browser file picker on the web)
  • Legal name (bookings and ID)
  • Preferred name (what the trip shows on chips and lists)
  • Birthdate
  • Sign-in email
  • Up to two other emails used on bookings (not extra logins)
  • Phone and home address

Signed-in accounts can change email and password here.

Travel Programs

  • Passport — country, number, issue and expiration; photos of the photo page and optional back (up to several passports).
  • Trusted Traveler — KTN, Global Entry, and similar; photos of the card front and optional back.
  • Loyalty Programs — airline (frequent flyer), hotel, rental car, cruise line, AAA.
  • Other — categories you name, with document photos (front and optional back). Use this for visas, vaccination cards, or anything that is not passport, trusted traveler, or loyalty.

Medical

Why this exists. On a trip you may need a member ID at a clinic, a doctor’s phone number, a photo of a prescription label to refill, or a short note about an allergy. Medical keeps that with your profile so you are not hunting through camera roll or a paper folder. It is not a shared family health record and not part of the itinerary other people download.

Who can see it. Only the person signed in on this account, on their own Profile. Trip owners, other travelers, family/friend viewers, and travel agents cannot open someone else’s Medical section. Saving another person’s passport or loyalty cannot change or replace their medical details.

Open Medical from Profile, or menu → Profile → Medical:

Photos are the pictures you attach. The app does not scan insurance cards or labels for text. Type member IDs and names yourself if you want them searchable as text.

Encryption and privacy

On a signed-in profile, passport numbers, trusted-traveler numbers, loyalty IDs, medical text, and the medical/document photos you attach are encrypted in the database with this traveler profile. That ciphertext is not copied into the offline itinerary database that other people on the trip sync.

In the product:

  • Casual reads of the itinerary never see passport, loyalty, or medical plaintext.
  • Family/friend viewers never receive those profile rows.
  • Medical is omitted unless you are looking at your own account.

In Basic (no account), the same kinds of fields stay on this device (phone or this browser) until you sign in. They are not in the cloud yet.

Encryption here is protection of the stored profile, not a hospital-grade record system and not a public share. You still choose what to type and which photos to attach.

Someone else’s profile on a trip

Owner, trip in Edit, menu → Travelers → Profile beside that person: personal, passport, and loyalty—not medical. Editors and viewers can open their own Profile from Travelers, not someone else’s. Invited people who have not signed in yet have no Profile button.

21. Family/friend viewers

Invited with Invite family/friend viewer. They sign in with that email and use the same traveler app.

They see: days, flights, hotels, times, confirmations, place/temps/currency, allowed cruise/train labels, Read Journal for notes shared with family/friends, trip photos (and comments) only when the share included family/friends.

They do not see: item notes, dock/tender, train overnight/station, passport, loyalty, medical, Travelers list, Journal Entry, packing/prep, import address, Nearby, New trip (while this is their only access).

Short profile: name, birthdate, email, password.

22. Reminders (phone)

Optional lock-screen alerts on iPhone and Android. Not in the browser. Family/friend viewers do not see the reminder line.

On Flight, Train, Coach/Bus, Rental car, Drive, Car service, Activity, Dining, Cruise: Enable reminder. Default 2 hours before a flight, 1 hour before other items; you can change the time. The day card shows whether a reminder is set.

Flight alerts can say to have your airline boarding pass ready when Airline is filled. This is not a stored boarding pass. Allow notifications when the phone asks.

23. Flight status overlay

Premium, Online. For a flight about 12 hours past to 48 hours ahead, the day screen may show a reported status (on time, delay, cancelled, diverted) and when it was heard. Booked times do not change. It is not official. Always confirm with the airline or airport screens. Family/friend viewers can see the line.

24. Nearby trip sync

Premium, phones only. Not on the website. Family/friend viewers cannot use it. Travelers already on the same trip can pass small updates over Bluetooth when there is no internet.

On iPhone, keep Bluetooth on and stay on the Nearby screen. Allow Bluetooth if asked.

  1. Bluetooth on (Airplane Mode is fine if Bluetooth is on). Do not start a hotspot.
  2. Owner: menu → About this App → Nearby trip sync → Start offline sync → choose the trip → Share my updates. Keep that screen open.
  3. Other travelers/editors: same trip on Nearby → when the owner’s phone appears, Get updates.
  4. Wait until the phone says updates were received (or already up to date). Then go back to Trips.

Stay a few feet apart. One owner can share with several phones, one after another. How nearby sync works in the menu walks through the same steps.

If you signed out and have no internet, sign in on that phone with the same email and password as last time so Premium (and Nearby) can unlock from data already on the device.

28. What this app does not do

  • Book or pay for flights, hotels, or cruises
  • Store a boarding pass
  • Replace airline apps or airport screens
  • Live weather forecasts or live currency conversion
  • Move a Basic trip between browser and phone without signing in
  • Nearby, lock-screen trip-activity notices, or trip photos in the browser
  • A public photo link or auto-post to Apple Photos / Google Photos
  • Agency or company-travel planner tools (separate sites)
  • Act as a medical device, electronic health record, or official insurance card. Keep originals.
  • Delete a trip for everyone when you only archive it (archive is yours alone)
