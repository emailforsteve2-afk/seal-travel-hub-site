Privacy Policy

SEAL Travel Hub · Effective October 21, 2026

Who we are

SEAL Travel Hub is a travel itinerary app from SEAL Team Designs. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Questions: sealteamdesigns@gmail.com.

Basic and Premium

On this phone, the app can run in Basic without an account. In Basic, trips and your traveler profile stay on the device. They are not uploaded to our servers until you create an account or sign in (Premium).

Premium uses an email and password so your trips can sync across devices, so you can invite other people, and so features such as booking import and nearby Bluetooth sync can work.

Information we collect

Account: email address, password (stored by our sign-in provider, not in readable form), and the name you enter.

Trips: titles, dates, destinations, day-by-day itinerary items (flights, hotels, and similar), notes, who you invite as a traveler or family/friend viewer, trip album photos you share, comments on those photos, journal notes you write (including notes marked Only me), travel-prep reminders, packing lists, and packing usuals you save.

Traveler profile: legal and preferred name, birthdate, phone, address, passport details, trusted traveler numbers (such as KTN or Global Entry), loyalty memberships, doctors, medical conditions you type, and insurance details you type.

Photos you choose to attach: a profile picture; medical insurance cards and prescription labels; passport and trusted-traveler cards; and other documents you store under Other. We do not scan those images for text. We store the picture you save.

Device and technical data needed to run the app: a local identifier on Basic installs, crash or error logs our hosts may record, signed-in session tokens, and (on iPhone and Android, in Premium) a push token so we can send lock-screen notices you have not turned off.

Medical and identity documents

Photos of insurance cards, prescription labels, passports, and similar IDs are sensitive. Medical details and medical photos are available only to the person signed in on that account. Trip owners can fill in passport and loyalty text for other travelers; they cannot open another person’s medical section.

You control what you photograph. Do not upload documents you are not allowed to store.

How we use information

To show your itinerary on this device and, in Premium, on other devices you sign in on.

To send invites you start. In Premium, to import booking confirmations you paste or forward, and to show optional flight-status and place photos.

To send lock-screen reminders you turn on, and (in Premium, on iPhone and Android) lock-screen notices for new trip photos, photo comments, and shared journal notes unless you turn those notices off.

To provide nearby Bluetooth trip updates when you use that feature, only with people on the same trip who are nearby.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your medical or passport photos for advertising.

Sharing

People you invite to a trip can see itinerary details allowed for their role. Family/friend viewers see a more limited plan (they do not see passport or loyalty details). In Premium, trip album photos you share are visible to the travelers and/or family/friend viewers you choose for that share. Comments on a photo are visible to the same people who can see that photo. Journal notes marked Only me stay with the person who wrote them. Shared journal notes follow the same traveler / family-friend choices as photos. If a photo file includes a location (from the camera when that setting is on), we may show a place name with the photo. We do not read your live GPS to tag a share.

We use infrastructure providers to host the app (including database, file storage, and optional AI extract when you use Premium import). They process data only to provide those services.

In Premium, place pictures on day cards are chosen once on our servers from Wikimedia Commons and stored with the trip.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Premium data stays until you delete it or delete your account. Delete my account in the menu removes your login, traveler profile (including stored photos), and trips you own. Trips you do not own remain for the other people on them; you are removed from those trips.

Basic data stays on this device until you delete trips or remove the app.

Children

SEAL Travel Hub is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13. You may store travel details for family members on your own account if you are allowed to do so.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page is the current version. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
