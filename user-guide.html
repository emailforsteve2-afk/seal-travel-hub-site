AS Travel Hub — User guide

For: People planning a trip for themselves, a family, or a small group

Covers: The Basic app (no account) and Premium (signed-in travel for phones and the web)

Not in this guide: Travel agencies, company/corporate travel desks, or product administration

Full reference: The traveler manual covers every traveler feature in detail. This file is the short starter.

AS Travel Hub is one traveler app. You start in Basic (no account). Signing in creates a login. Premium is a license on that login (already granted for current testers; otherwise a one-time code from the product administrator).

Your trips live on the device first. In Basic they stay only on the phone or the browser where you created them. A Basic trip in a web browser cannot be copied, transferred, or opened on a phone. The only way to use that trip on a phone is to Sign in or create account on that browser so the trip can leave it, then sign in on the phone. Signed-in copies can sync when you are online. Upgrade to Premium is a later step (a code, until store billing exists) and is not the same as creating a login.

Part 1 — Basic (no account)

What Basic is

AS Travel App (basic) is a private itinerary on this phone or this browser. You do not create an account. Nothing is shared with other people or other devices.

Browser and phone are not connected in Basic. If you build a trip in a web browser, that trip lives only in that browser. There is no export, email, Bluetooth, QR, or other unsigned Basic path to put it on an iPhone or Android. You must Sign in or create account on the same browser first; after the trip is on your account, sign in on the phone and wait until it is Online. The same is true in reverse: a Basic trip on a phone does not appear in a browser until you sign in on that phone.

Use Basic when you want to plan on one device, keep documents and medical notes with you, and optionally upgrade later.

Open the menu (three lines) → About this App to see the Basic version (for example Basic 1.0.11).

What you can do

  • Create trips: General, Cruise, Train, Road trip, or Flight
  • Build a day-by-day plan: flights (including legs and airline), hotels, trains, coaches, cruise items, activities, dining, and notes
  • Switch a trip between View and Edit (you own every Basic trip)
  • Paste a confirmation to fill a draft from booking text on the device, then review before saving
  • Keep a Profile: personal details, travel programs (airline, hotel, car, cruise, AAA), medical (insurance, doctors, medications, conditions), passport and trusted-traveler fields, and a photo on this device
  • Hide a trip under Archived trips and restore it later
  • Optional lock-screen reminders on the phone for flights and other timed items (not in the browser)
  • On iPhone and Android, tap a phone number in an item’s Notes to Call or Copy

Day cards can show a city, typical high/low, and local currency from where you are that day (for example the first real departure city of a flight day, not a same-day layover). Hawaii island temperatures can fill in without internet; other places may use the network only to look up a city name—not to store your trip in the cloud.

What Basic does not do

  • Sign in, invites, or sharing with family
  • Email import address (forward a confirmation to a trip mailbox)
  • Nearby Bluetooth sync between phones
  • Moving a trip from a browser to a phone (or from a phone to a browser) without signing in — there is no unsigned Basic way to do this
  • The same trip appearing on another phone or computer
  • Printing the formatted itinerary (that is Premium)
  • Sharing trip photos with travelers or family (that is Premium on the phone)

Empty home: No trips on this phone yet. Tap New trip to add one.

Menu

  • Profile → Personal, Travel Programs, Medical
  • Archived trips
  • About this App
  • Sign in or create account (unsigned). After a login, if the license is still Basic: Upgrade to Premium

There is no Nearby list, Travelers list, or Sign out in unsigned Basic.

Sign in, then Upgrade to Premium

Sign in or create account creates a login or signs you in on this device. That login is Basic unless an admin grant or one-time code has already set Premium. Trips already here (including a trip you only ever built in this browser) can attach to that account and sync when you are online. After you are signed in with a Basic license, Upgrade to Premium asks for a one-time Premium code (store purchase is not in the app yet). To use a browser-only unsigned trip on a phone: sign in in that browser, then sign in on the phone and wait until it is Online. Not now returns to unsigned Basic.

After you sign out, this device is Basic again. Profile details stay on the phone so Personal is not blank. Itineraries you already downloaded can stay on the device. Sign in later with the same email and password on this phone: with internet you reach the cloud; without internet the phone can still unlock that saved account so trips you already had (and Nearby, if you use it) can work. A new email needs internet at least once.

Part 2 — Premium (family and group travel)

Premium is the signed-in traveler app: iPhone, Android, and the traveler website. Phone and web are separate copies of your data until that copy is Online and has synced. A trip you see in the browser does not appear on a phone until that phone has signed in and finished downloading.

Family/friend people at home use the same traveler app with the invite email. They follow the itinerary; they are not listed as travelers.

Accounts

Create an account with name, email, and password, or sign in. Use the same email you were invited with so the trip can attach.

You can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in screen (email link). On a phone, that link is meant to open the app. On the web, it returns to the website. You can change sign-in email and password from Profile.

The home heading is Your trips. The menu About line shows the app version and build on a phone.

A badge shows Online, Downloading… / Uploading…, or Offline. Until a phone is Online, it only shows trips already stored on that phone. Retry sync if it stays offline when you expect a connection.

Your trips

Cards are trips you travel on and trips you only follow. Follow-only cards are labeled Family/friend viewer.

New trip (not shown if you only follow someone else’s trip): title, optional destination, type, optional dates. You become owner. Dates create the day list.

Archived trips hides a trip from the main list for you; Archive trip and Restore trip are on the trip’s days list in Edit. Deleting a trip (owner, in Edit) removes it for everyone on that trip.

On a wide web window, trip cards can sit two across.

View and Edit

Owners and editors open a trip in Viewing. Tap View | Edit to Editing when you need to change days, items, or import. Invite travelers and family/friend viewers from the menu without switching to Edit. People who can only read never see that switch.

Search days and activities matches dates, places, titles, flight paths, confirmation numbers, times, and (for travelers, not family/friend viewers) notes.

Print itinerary is in the menu (three lines) under About this App, while you are in a trip—not on Your trips. It opens the phone or browser print dialog with a one-trip document: title, destination, dates, travelers (not shown to family/friend viewers), then each day with place, typical temps, currency, cruise/train facts, and items. Family/friend viewers get the same itinerary they already see (no item notes). Closing the iPhone print sheet without printing is fine; no error. Printed pages use about a 0.6 inch margin.

Print journal is on that same menu. It threads every journal note you are allowed to see, by day, with the writer’s name. Your private Only me notes are included on your copy and marked that way. Other people’s private notes never appear.

Set up a trip yourself

You do not need an agency. One owner can create the trip, load bookings, then invite others.

  1. On Your trips, tap New trip. Give it a title, pick a type (General, Cruise, Train, Road trip, or Flight), and optional destination and dates. Dates create the day list. You become owner.
  2. Open the trip. Switch View | Edit to Editing whenever you add or change days, items, or imports.
  3. Load the itinerary in any mix of these ways:

- Type it — open a day (or Add itinerary item) and enter flights, hotels, trains, and the rest by hand.

- Paste a confirmation — in Edit, tap Import Booking Confirmation Email. Paste the booking or sailing email, run extract, review the draft, then save.

- Forward the confirmation — in Edit, the trip shows Forward a confirmation and a unique email address. Copy address, then forward the entire airline, hotel, train, or cruise email to that address (not a screenshot of the subject line). When the app has it, Booking to review appears. Open Import, check the draft, then save—or Remove if it is the wrong mail.

  1. Invite travelers and family/friend viewers from the menu when you are ready. Importing a name from a booking does not email them; you still invite.

Nothing from paste or forward is written onto the trip until you confirm the draft. Undo last import (Edit) removes the last batch if you need to try again.

How extract works

When you are Online, paste and forwarded mail go through the AI itinerary extractor (extract-booking). It reads the confirmation and proposes flights (including legs), hotels, trains, cruises, times, confirmation numbers, and traveler names. You always review that proposal. If you are offline, or AI is unavailable, a built-in reader on the device fills a simpler draft instead. Either way, check times, airports, and names before you save.

Forward the whole message, not a snippet. One trip address is only for this trip—do not reuse it for a different vacation. Family/friend viewers never see the import address or the review list.

Travel Prep Reminders and Packing List (Premium)

Open a trip, then the three-line menu. Travel Prep Reminders, Packing List, and Packing usuals are for travelers only—not family/friend viewers. They live in the app; they do not send lock-screen alerts.

Travel Prep Reminders

A to-do list before you leave.

  • This trip — shared chores (hold mail, confirm reservations). Owners and editors check these off.
  • For you — your own reminders (passport still valid, refill a prescription). Only you check these off.

The app adds a few suggestions from the itinerary. You can type your own, pick from a short catalog, or Remove a line you do not need. Suggestions are not official passport, visa, or insurance advice—confirm those yourself.

Done takes you back to the menu.

Packing List

What goes in the bag. Days and nights for this trip show at the top.

Each traveler has their own list, split into Carry-On Bag and Checked Bag. Tap a bag at the top to switch. Passport / ID, medications, chargers, and toiletries start in carry-on; clothes start in checked. Tap Move to Carry-On or Move to Checked under an item to switch luggage. On a narrow phone the item name sits on the first line and Target / Packed / Complete-or-Missing on the second so nothing is clipped.

Owners and editors add a kids list (one per child, with age) at the bottom of the packing page, under Your bags. If that child is no longer going, tap Remove list on that kids list. Your own bags cannot be deleted this way.

Each line has:

  • the item name
  • Target — how many you plan to bring (+/− or tap the number)
  • Packed — how many are already in that luggage
  • Complete or Missing — Packed has reached Target, or how many are still short

The line is done when Packed is at least Target. Tap × to drop an item. A short Missing from… note lists anything still short in that bag.

At the bottom of a bag: Add Item to type a name, Add from catalog to check extra items, or Add all … usuals for that bag’s always-bring list. Edit carry-on usuals / Edit checked bag usuals (also Packing usuals in the menu) opens a separate screen to build those two lists from the catalog or by typing a name with a category. A typed usual is also saved to your catalog.

Typed items ask for a category (Documents, Clothes, Health, …). The app may pre-select one from the name (for example dental floss → Health); you can change it. That item is saved to your catalog for every future trip, not only this one. Built-in catalog lines stay for everyone; your extras are yours. In the catalog, tap × next to one of yours to drop it from the catalog.

Packing usuals

A separate screen (teal-gray cards) for items you always bring. There are two lists: Carry-On usuals and Checked usuals. Build them from the catalog or by typing a name with a category (that also adds to your catalog). On Packing List, with a bag selected, Add or Add all … usuals drops only that bag’s usuals onto this trip.

Days and items

Each calendar day is Day 1, Day 2, … from the trip start. Cruise and Train trips can also show Cruise Day or Train Day so boarding can start after fly-in days.

In Edit, open a day and tap the facts on the right to set a day title (Travel Day, Beach day, Day trip to Boston). That title shows on the trip’s day cards and at the top of the day. The overnight city (Wells, Boston, …) stays separate.

Opening a day shows date, typical high/low, currency, a place photo when a free-to-use one is found, and (on cruise/train trips) ship or train times. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons or Openverse (CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain, not fair use). Tap the i on the photo for creator, source, and license links. At Sea days use a cruise-ship photo instead of a city. Docked or Tender and train overnight/station facts are hidden from family/friend viewers.

On a flight in the next two days (Premium, while Online), a status line may appear under the booked times: possible delay, cancelled, or on time, as reported. Booked times do not change. Always confirm with the airline or airport screens.

Journal (Premium)

Read Journal on the trip days list opens every note you may see, grouped by day, with the writer’s name. That screen is for reading only (filters All, Mine, Shared). To write or change a note, open that day and tap Journal Entry. Family/friend viewers see Read Journal on the trip; they do not see Journal Entry on a day.

On a day, Journal Entry and Photos sit above Activities & Details. All days in Journal Entry goes back to Read Journal. In Edit, Add itinerary item on the activities heading adds a flight, hotel, or other itinerary row—not a journal note or photo.

Owners and editors can write. Check Only me for a private note (default), or Travelers and/or Family/friends to share. The last share choice is remembered on this device. You can edit or remove your own notes later.

Travelers and family/friend viewers see notes shared with them. Traveler-viewers do not write journal notes. Only me notes stay with the writer.

On iPhone and Android, a shared journal note can send a lock-screen notice to people who can see it (not the writer). Turn that off under the menu → Display → Lock screen for Photos, comments, and journal. The phone’s Focus, quiet hours, and mute still apply. Journal works on the traveler website; lock-screen notices do not.

Trip photos (Premium, iPhone and Android)

Trip photos live on the trip, next to the days, not in a group chat and not as a public link. They are not on the traveler website.

Photos on the trip page (and on a day) opens the album.

Share (travelers only — owners, editors, and traveler-viewers):

  1. Check Travelers and/or Family/friends. The last choice is remembered on this phone.
  2. Optionally type a caption (the same caption for up to 12 pictures in one batch).
  3. Tap Choose from camera roll and pick the pictures.

You do not pick a day. The app groups each picture under a trip day from when it was taken (or from when you shared it, if the file has no date). Pictures that do not land on a trip day sit under Other photos.

If the picture file includes a location (Camera or Photos location is on), a place name can appear on its own line under the caption. The app does not read your live GPS. If location is off in the phone’s camera settings, there is no place name.

Look: the album is a story, Day 1 then Day 2, and so on. Open a picture to see the place name from the photo file, then any caption, then the trip day and date (not the day-card city). Swipe left or right for the next picture. Anyone who can see it can Save to camera roll. The person who shared it can Edit caption (or add or clear one). The person who shared it, or an owner or editor, can Remove it. Travelers and family/friend viewers who can see the picture can type comments or questions in the thread under it. A number on a thumbnail shows how many comments it has (teal if you have not read them). Photos puts pictures with new comments at the top.

Notice: opening a trip can show a popup when there are new photos or comments you have not seen. Open Photos or Later. Turn the popup off under the menu → Display. On iPhone and Android, the same menu has Lock screen for Photos, comments, and journal. Turn that off to stop lock-screen notices from this app. Your phone’s Focus, quiet hours, and mute still apply when it is on.

Family/friend viewers see only photos shared with family/friends or both. They cannot share.

Activities & Details hold:

  • Title and category: Flight, Hotel, Rental Car, Drive, Car service, Train, Coach/Bus, Cruise, Activity, Dining, or Note
  • Times at that place (not converted for another time zone)
  • Drive: One-way (ending city moves where later days are) or Round trip (does not move the city)
  • Car service: Booked car, limo, taxi, or rideshare. Pickup → drop-off in location. Does not move the city
  • Who it is for (everyone, or named travelers, including people still invited)
  • Confirmation, location, flight legs
  • Notes (travelers only; on the phone, tap a number to Call or Copy)
  • Optional reminder on the phone (flights default to 2 hours before; others 1 hour). Flight reminders can name the airline so you open the airline app for a boarding pass. Reminders do not appear in the browser.

On the web, a wide window can show several day cards across. On a day, Previous day / Next day sit at the bottom.

Overnight cruise, multi-night hotel, and rental-car items use blank clocks on the display so check-in, check-out, pickup, and drop-off days read clearly.

Open that trip, then the menu (three lines) → Travelers. That list shows people on this trip by email and role. It appears on the phone and on the website while you are in the trip. Family/friend viewers do not see it.

Traveler profile (Premium)

From the menu: photo, legal name (bookings and ID), preferred name (what the trip shows), birthdate, sign-in email, up to two other emails used on bookings (not extra logins), phone, address, passport, KTN / Global Entry, loyalty programs, and your medical details (insurance, doctors, medications, conditions). A booking that uses a nickname still counts as you. Sensitive numbers are stored for your account, not copied into the shared day-by-day itinerary that family/friend viewers sync.

A trip owner can open another traveler’s profile on that trip to help keep documents together. Switch the trip to Edit, open the menu → Travelers, then tap Profile beside that person. That screen is personal details, passport, and loyalty—not medical. Medical is only on the Profile menu of the person who is signed in. Editors and viewers can open their own Profile from the Travelers list, not someone else’s. People who have been invited but have not signed in yet do not have a Profile button.

Someone who only follows as family/friend uses a short profile: name, birthdate, email, password.

Photos: on the phone, pick from the library. On the web, use the browser file picker (it behaves differently from the phone).

Nearby trip sync (phones only)

When there is no internet, travelers already on the same trip can pass small updates between phones. Family/friend viewers cannot use Nearby. This is not on the website.

On iPhone, keep Bluetooth on and stay on the Nearby screen. If iOS asks to use Bluetooth, allow it.

  1. Bluetooth on (Airplane Mode is fine if Bluetooth is on). Do not start a hotspot.
  2. Owner: menu → Nearby trip sync → choose the trip → Share my updates. Keep that screen open.
  3. Other travelers/editors: same trip on Nearby → when the owner’s phone appears, Get updates.
  4. Wait until the phone says updates were received (or already up to date). Then go back to Trips.

Stay a few feet apart. One owner can share with several phones, one after another. How nearby sync works in the app walks through the same steps.

If you signed out and have no internet, sign in on that phone with the same email and password as last time so Premium (and Nearby) can unlock from data already on the device.

Web-only notes (Premium traveler site)

The website is the same trip, days, people, import (paste and inbound email), print, and profiles—in a browser.

  • Use a wide window for extra columns of trip and day cards.
  • Print itinerary and Print journal use the browser print dialog (you can cancel without an error).
  • No Bluetooth Nearby.
  • No lock-screen reminders and no lock-screen notices for photos, comments, or journal.
  • No trip photo album (share, comments, and save are phone only). Journal notes still work in the browser.
  • Notes do not offer Call/Copy; they stay as text.
  • Photo attach uses the browser, not the phone library.
  • Phone and web stay separate until each one is Online and synced.

Typical uses

A family cruise. One person creates a cruise trip, invites a spouse as editor, forwards flight emails to the trip address, adds hotels on fly-in days, sets Cruise Day 1 on embarkation, and assigns a shore excursion to one person. From the phones, they share trip photos with travelers and family/friends and leave comments on a picture; they write a shared journal note from a day. A parent at home is a family/friend viewer, sees flight times without item notes or passport numbers, opens Read Journal for shared notes, and opens Photos to follow the story, read comments, and save a picture to their camera roll. Shared photos, comments, and journal notes can appear on a traveler’s lock screen.

Two people, different flights. Each sees their city and typical temps on that calendar day.

Planning on the computer, traveling with the phone. Build and print on the web while Online. Open the same account on the phone, wait until Online, then use the itinerary (and reminders or Nearby) on the trip.
