SEAL Travel Hub

SEAL Travel Hub brings your travel information together in one place and organizes it into a day-by-day itinerary.

You can:

  • Keep flights, hotels, cruises, trains, and activities in a single itinerary
  • Store confirmation numbers, reservation details, and travel notes
  • Share trips with other travelers
  • View your itinerary from your phone or computer
  • Create journals and save trip photos
  • Create packing lists and travel reminders

SEAL Travel Hub helps you organize travel you have already booked.

⚠️ Important: It does not book travel, and it does not connect to live travel provider statuses (such as flight times/updates/statuses).

What These Instructions Cover

This guide explains how to create an account, create a trip, and populate it with your booked travel details.

You will learn how to:

  1. Create an account and sign in.
  2. Create a new trip.
  3. Import booking confirmations.
  4. Automatically add travel details to your itinerary.
  5. Add additional reservations, activities, and notes.

Step 1: Create an Account

You need a SEAL Travel Hub account before you can use the app or the website.

  1. On the sign-in screen:
  2. Tap or click Create account.
  3. Enter your email address, your name, and a password.
  4. Tap or click Create account again to finish.

If you already have an account, tap or click Sign in instead.

Step 2: Create a New Trip

  1. Click Create New Trip.
  2. Enter a Trip Title.
  3. Leave Trip Type as General if you're unsure.
  4. Optionally enter a Start Date.
  5. Click Save Trip.

You can leave blank and let the imported booking confirmations set and update the trip dates automatically.

Step 3: Open the Trip

  1. Select the trip you just created.
  2. Click Edit.

You are now ready to import bookings.

Step 4: Import Booking Confirmations to automatically add travel details your itinerary.

Option A: Forward the Confirmation Email

  1. Copy the trip's import email address shown on the screen.
  2. Forward your booking confirmation email to that address.
  3. Wait for import processing to complete.

⏱️Note: Most booking confirmations are processed in 30 seconds to 1 minute.

⚠️ Important: Every trip gets its own unique email address for importing bookings. When you forward a booking confirmation to that email address, SEAL Travel Hub adds the information to that specific trip. Sending a booking confirmation to the wrong trip email address may cause that booking to be imported into the wrong trip.

Option B: Copy and Paste the Confirmation

  1. Open the booking confirmation email or PDF.
  2. Copy the text.
  3. Click Import Booking Confirmation Email.
  4. Paste the text and start the import.

Step 5: Review the Results

When processing is complete, you'll see a message such as "Extracted with the Booking Graph" or "Extracted with OpenAI". These messages indicate that the booking information has been extracted and is ready for review.

Review the extracted information and import it into your trip.

Step 6: Import Additional Bookings

Most trips include multiple reservations and these also can be imported. For example:

  1. Import a cruise confirmation.
  2. Import flight confirmations.
  3. Import hotel confirmations.
  4. Import any transfers, trains, rental cars, or excursions.

SEAL Travel Hub combines everything into a single itinerary.

Step 7: Complete Your Itinerary

After importing your bookings, add any additional:

  • Activities
  • Excursions
  • Restaurant reservations
  • Notes
  • Reminders
  • Travel contacts

You can update your itinerary any time: before, during, and after your trip.

Quick Example

  1. Create an account (or sign in).
  2. Create a trip called Alaska Cruise 2027.
  3. Open the trip and click Edit.
  4. Forward your cruise confirmation email to the trip's import email address.
  5. Wait about 30 seconds to 1 minute for processing until the screen prompts the next step.
  6. Review and import the results.
  7. Repeat the process for flights, hotels, and other reservations.
  8. Add activities, reservations, and notes.

Your itinerary is now ready to use.
