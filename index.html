SEAL Travel Hub

Your trip, day by day — on the phone you already carry.

SEAL Travel Hub is a travel organizer for people planning their own trips, a family trip, or a small group. Keep flights, hotels, trains, cruises, rentals, activities, dining, and notes in one itinerary. It runs on iPhone, Android, and the traveler website.

Basic — no account. Plan on this phone (or this browser) only. Trips stay on the device.

Premium — sign in. Invite traveling companions, let family at home follow along, sync when you are online, and keep using the itinerary offline.

What you can do

  • Build a day-by-day plan: general, cruise, train, road trip, or flight-centered trips.
  • Record legs, times, confirmation numbers, and who each item is for.
  • Paste a booking confirmation and review it before anything is saved.
  • Keep a traveler profile: names, loyalty programs, passport, and (on your own profile) medical notes.
  • Optional lock-screen reminders on the phone (not in the browser).
  • Invite others as owner, editor, or viewer — or as a family/friend follower who is not listed as a traveler.

What it does not do

  • It does not book flights, hotels, or cruises, and it does not take payments.
  • It does not replace the airline app. Any flight status line is unofficial; always confirm with the airline.
  • A Basic trip in a web browser cannot move to a phone until you sign in (Upgrade to Premium) on that browser.

Getting started on iPhone

Open the app. You can use it immediately in Basic. To use Premium: open the menu (three lines) → Upgrade to Premium → create an account or sign in.
